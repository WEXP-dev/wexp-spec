# IETF artifact legal status

Internet-Drafts and other IETF Contributions in this repository remain subject
to the applicable IETF contribution and document regime, including BCP 78 /
RFC 5378 and the applicable IETF Trust Legal Provisions.

Copyright, license, and other legal notices embedded in each IETF artifact are
authoritative for that artifact. Repository import, hashing, integrity
manifests, GitHub storage, or WEXP witnessing do not change the artifact's
legal status.

Code Components contained in IETF Documents remain subject to the applicable
IETF Trust provisions for Code Components. Repository-authored material outside
IETF specification artifacts is not automatically relicensed merely because it
is stored alongside an Internet-Draft.

Consult the authoritative sources rather than a copied legal text:

- [RFC 5378 / BCP 78](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5378.html)
- [Current and archived IETF Trust Legal Provisions](https://trustee.ietf.org/documents/trust-legal-provisions/)

This notice explains how IETF legal terms apply to material in this repository.
It is not legal advice and does not replace the notices in an individual IETF
document.
